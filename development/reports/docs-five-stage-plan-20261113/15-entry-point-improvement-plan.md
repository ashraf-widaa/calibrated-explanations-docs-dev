## 1. Shared standards (all entry points)

- **Landing-page pattern.** Title (unchanged) · one-paragraph purpose and
  audience · "Start here" (one recommended first page) · grouped cards with
  one-line descriptions · "Levels in this section" (which pages are L1, L2,
  L3) · "Where else to go" (two to four cross-entry links) · semantics pointer
  where STD-004 requires it.
- **Page navigation block.** Next · Go deeper · See also · Before this
  (`13` §5), on every content page, not only the Quick Start.
- **Tables on phones.** Tables with more than four columns get a phone
  alternative: either split into two narrower tables, or a definition list
  for the phone layout (CSS in `docs/_static/ce-docs.css`, scoped to
  `.ce-table-stack`); the 15 scrolling tables above are the work list.
- **Figures.** Standard of `docs/_static/figures/ce-in-ml-workflow.svg`:
  transparent background, text inside filled shapes, blue = conventional /
  orange = added by CE (palette validated for light, dark and colour-vision
  deficiency), `<title>`/`<desc>` + alt text, narrow variant when labels would
  fall below ~11 px at 390 px, and a **provenance tag** in the caption: "CE
  output (generated by …)" or "Schematic".
- **Diagrams as code.** Mermaid diagrams are replaced by accessible SVG
  where they teach a concept (they render without alt text and change with the
  theme).

## 3. Entry-point recommendations

### 3.1 Quick Start — orientation hub (WP-1; detail in `14`)

- **Purpose/audience:** everyone's first stop; big picture without code.
- **Landing:** keep definition, figure (D-16), A/B/C cards, order, "Before you
  start" (install as a link), add "Where should I go next?".
- **Hierarchy:** A1, A2, B (*CE by example*, generated, code-free), C1, C2
  (*Apply CE: the practical workflow*).
- **Depth:** L1 throughout, L2 orientation (what each step produces) without
  code; L3 only by links.
- **Visuals:** V-1, V-3, V-5, V-11.
- **Accessibility/mobile:** C1's two wide tables restructured (capability
  table → phone stack); A1 mermaid → SVG.

### 3.2 CE: What & Why — compendium, L1 (WP-2, WP-5, **WP-13**)

- **Purpose/audience:** decision-makers, students, newcomers: why trust is
  hard, what CE adds, how it compares, the regulatory context.
- **Landing intro (WP-13):** two short paragraphs (problem, CE's answer);
  "Start here: Black boxes and explainable AI"; levels map.
- **Hierarchy (ordered):** Understanding AI models (→ A1) → Black boxes and
  XAI [N] → Can we trust AI outputs? (→ `calibration_intuition`) → What CE is
  → The explainability gap (`compare`, rewritten) → Regulation context.
- **Depth:** L1 owners; L3 via `calibrated_interval_semantics`,
  `calibration_methods`.
- **Visuals:** V-2 (schematic comparison), V-1 reuse.
- **Regulation card (WP-13):** relabel the card/sidebar entry "Regulation
  context (EU AI Act)" and state in its description that it is a detailed
  practitioner guide; its claims (line 478) go to the author sheet (WP-8).
  The page itself stays in place (URL unchanged).
- **Accessibility/mobile:** what-is-ce's wide table → phone stack.

### 3.3 How CE Works — compendium, L1→L3 (WP-3, WP-4, WP-9, **WP-14**)

  guarantees.
- **Landing intro (WP-14):** "Read in this order" list (intuition →
  workflow → methods → guarantees → explanation construction → reading
  explanations) with a level tag per page; one sentence on what L3 means
  here (definitions, derivations, references).
- **Hierarchy:** as `02` rev. 2 §2.3 (reorder, relabel; URLs unchanged).
- **Depth:** keep all advanced material where it is (`calibration_methods`,
  semantics, `explanation_structures`).
- **Visuals:** V-4, V-6, V-7, V-8.
- **Content:** `interpret_explanations` §4 (telemetry) → one "See also" link
  to the telemetry how-to; worked numbers in `interpret_explanations` aligned

- **Accessibility/mobile:** `calibration_methods` (4 wide tables, 13 px
  table text) and `ce_workflow` (2 wide tables, learning-path table) →
  phone stacks; keep MathJax as is.
