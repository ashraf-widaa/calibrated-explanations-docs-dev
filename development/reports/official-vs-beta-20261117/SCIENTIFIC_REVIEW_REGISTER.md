## Statistical validity assessment
**Validity verdict: valid with caveats as a documentation review candidate.** This is not a verdict proving every CE method or every public guarantee. The documentation increasingly distinguishes implementation behavior, illustrative results and theoretical statements; the open register identifies the remaining bridges between them.
**Assumptions check.** Exchangeability is stated but cannot be established for a future user dataset. Proper-training/calibration/test roles are explicit; preprocessing and fitted difficulty estimators must follow the appropriate fixed-component protocol. Data drift and adaptive calibration choices remain boundary conditions.
**Coverage analysis.** Standard marginal coverage is not per-input coverage or conditional-on-acceptance accuracy. Predefined Mondrian categories require the relevant within-category assumptions; they do not confer arbitrary subgroup or pointwise guarantees. The VA pair, merged probability and rescaled multiclass outputs require separate language.
**Calibration protocol review.** The event construction divides supplied calibration data between CPS scoring and VA calibration. Per-group counts, percentile resolution and fixed preprocessing matter. No universal minimum sample-size recommendation is inferred from this report.
**Finite-sample concerns.** Authors should verify ties, smoothing, finite endpoint availability, requested percentile conventions and one-sided display handling. Small groups can give uninformative endpoints; tests showing output shapes do not establish coverage.
**Conditional-coverage caveats.** Explanation-weight ranges and ensured filtering have no newly proved coverage statement here. Group-conditioned methods must not be described as guaranteeing correctness for each patient or accepted decision.
**Likely reviewer objections.** Pair/merged-probability language can overstate validity; a width criterion can be over-interpreted; future-version prose can outrun the implementation; application/legal examples can imply sufficiency beyond CE; long methods material needs human educational review.
**Recommended fixes after feedback.** Resolve the specific statements in the table, attach implementing commits or theorem conditions where requested, then make narrow source-consistent corrections on a future authorized branch. This task makes no documentation corrections.
